# TechSuite Privacy Policy

_Last Updated: September 4, 2025_

## At a Glance
- We do not sell your personal data.  
- We use information only to provide and improve TechSuite.  
- Some data may be used in anonymized or aggregated form with AI systems to make TechSuite smarter and more reliable.  
- We use trusted providers like AWS (hosting) and Stripe (billing) to operate securely.  
- You can contact us anytime to request account deletion or to exercise rights under privacy laws.  

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### a. Website Visitors
- You may browse most of our website without providing personal data.  
- If you register for an account, forum, or newsletter, we collect the information you provide (such as name, email, and login credentials).  
- We use cookies and analytics tools to measure traffic and improve performance. This may include IP addresses, cookie identifiers, and activity on our site. You can opt out via [DAA](https://www.aboutads.info/), [NAI](https://www.networkadvertising.org/choices/), or [EDAA](https://youronlinechoices.eu/) in Europe.  

### b. TechSuite Users
When you use TechSuite, the software may collect and upload:  
- Hardware and operating system specifications  
- Diagnostic reports generated by TechSuite  
- Logs and performance metrics  
- Debug information in case of crashes or errors  
- IP address and account identifiers (to manage licensing and prevent unauthorized use)  

### c. Billing Information
We do not store payment card details. All billing is securely processed by **Stripe**, a PCI-compliant provider.  

### d. Support Interactions
If you contact us for support, we may collect additional logs, screenshots, or communications you provide. These may be stored to improve service and track recurring issues.  

---

## 2. How We Use Information
We use collected information to:  
- Provide, maintain, and improve TechSuite  
- Detect and prevent misuse or unauthorized access  
- Troubleshoot and resolve technical issues  
- Communicate with you about updates, support, and account matters  
- Analyze usage patterns to understand how TechSuite is applied in real environments  
- Enhance TechSuite with new features, including AI-powered diagnostics  
- Create anonymized, aggregated reports about industry trends (e.g., hardware failure rates, malware trends). Any public reports will not include personal or customer-identifiable data.  

---

## 3. Use of AI
We may use diagnostic and usage data in **anonymized or aggregated form** with AI systems to improve TechSuite. Examples include:  
- Training models to better identify hardware faults or software issues  
- Summarizing logs to speed up technician workflows  

We do not use customer content outside the scope of TechSuite, and we do not sell data for AI training unrelated to TechSuite.  

---

## 4. Sharing of Information
- We do not sell or rent your personal data.  
- We may share anonymized, aggregated data for research or industry reporting.  
- We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights, users, or the public.  
- We may share limited data with trusted service providers — including **AWS** (hosting), **Stripe** (billing), and analytics providers — who process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.  

---

## 5. Data Storage, Transfers, and Security
- Your information may be stored and processed in the United States or other countries where Sacramento Labs or its service providers operate.  
- We apply reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational measures to protect your data, including **encryption at rest and in transit**, and internal **access controls** so only employees or contractors with a need-to-know basis can access user data.  

---

## 6. Data Retention and Your Choices
- Diagnostic and log data are retained as long as needed to provide support and improve TechSuite, and may then be anonymized or deleted.  
- Account information is retained while your account is active, and may be deleted upon request.  
- You may request deletion of your account, logs, or other data by contacting us.  
- You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link provided.  

---

## 7. Regulatory Alignment
We align our practices with applicable privacy laws such as the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** and the **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)**. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as the right to access, correct, or delete your information.  

---

## 8. Children’s Privacy
TechSuite is intended for professional use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under 16. If you believe such data has been collected, please contact us.  

---

## 9. Updates to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last Updated” date above indicates the most recent revision. If material changes are made, we will provide notice (for example, by email or website notice).
